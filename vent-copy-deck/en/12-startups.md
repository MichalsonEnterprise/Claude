## SECTION 06 — FOUNDER FAQ

**Q: Does applying mean I'm raising from VENT?**
A: No. VENT is not a fund today, and research is not a funding process. Applying puts your company through screening and research in an ecosystem where a separate future vehicle, VENT Ventures I, is planned. No stage of the process is an investment commitment. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: What does it cost?**
A: Standard applications are free, always. Accelerated Review is a paid service that shortens the queue without touching criteria or outcomes.

**Q: Can I pay for a better score?**
A: No — and if anyone offers that, report it. Scores come from the published methodology; payment affects scheduling only.

**Q: What if my application is declined?**
A: You receive the decision with reasons and, where applicable, what would need to change for reapplication. Declined isn't blacklisted: companies can reapply after [PLACEHOLDER: period].

**Q: What happens to confidential information?**
A: Application data is handled under our Privacy Policy and confidentiality terms. Published profiles contain only material you approve for publication; sensitive diligence data stays gated. [PLACEHOLDER: confidentiality terms link] `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: Do I control what's published?**
A: You approve the public profile scope before it goes live. Research conclusions and scores remain independent — approval covers disclosure, not editorial content.

**Q: What does "Investment Ready" mean for me?**
A: That your research and review met the pipeline's quality bar. It signals standard, not endorsement — and it creates no right or promise of investment. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

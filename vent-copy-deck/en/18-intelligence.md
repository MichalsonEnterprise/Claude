## SECTION 05 — EDITORIAL MANIFESTO

**Eyebrow:**
EDITORIAL STANDARDS

**Headline:**
What this publication will and won't do.

**Manifesto list:**
1. **No sponsored coverage disguised as analysis.** Paid placements don't exist here; founder services never buy editorial treatment.
2. **Methodology disclosed.** Claims carry sources; models state assumptions; estimates are labelled estimates.
3. **Corrections published.** Material errors are fixed visibly, with a change note — not silently rewritten.
4. **Authors are accountable.** Every piece carries a named author and their Reputation record.
5. **No price commentary on VENT.** This publication analyzes markets and companies. It does not promote the ecosystem's own token. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`
6. **Written up, never down.** The reader is assumed intelligent. Hype is a category error.

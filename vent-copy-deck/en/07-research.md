## SECTION 03 — RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

**Eyebrow:**
METHODOLOGY

**Headline:**
Same questions, every company.

**Body:**
Every profile is built through the same sequence: structured founder input, independent verification of claims where feasible, market and competitive analysis, technology and IP examination, and editorial review before publication. Sources are cited; assumptions are labelled; what we couldn't verify is stated as exactly that.

**Method steps:**
1. **Intake** — Structured application data and founder interviews.
2. **Verification** — Claims checked against sources; unverifiable claims flagged, not repeated.
3. **Analysis** — Market, product, business model, competition, funding context.
4. **Technology & IP** — Defensibility examination through IP Intelligence powered by PatentChain.
5. **Community input** — Structured member assessment, aggregated and attributed as community signal.
6. **Editorial review** — Second-analyst review against the published standard before release.

## SECTION 04 — SCORE METHODOLOGY

**Eyebrow:**
SCORING

**Headline:**
Eight published scores. Zero black boxes.

**Body:**
Each full profile publishes eight scores: seven scored dimensions — Market through IP — and the VENT Score (0–100), their weighted composite. Every dimension shows its rationale — a score you can disagree with is a score you can use.

**Score cards:**
- **Market Score** — Size, structure, timing, and accessibility of the opportunity.
- **Team Score** — Capability, completeness, and evidence of execution.
- **Product Score** — Product quality, differentiation, and user evidence.
- **Traction Score** — Verified commercial and usage momentum.
- **Risk Score** — Severity and coverage of identified risks (higher = lower risk profile).
- **Community Score** — Aggregated structured assessment from the member network.
- **IP Score** — Technology defensibility, via IP Intelligence powered by PatentChain.

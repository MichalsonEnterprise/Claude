## SECTION 02 — THE PROBLEM

**Eyebrow:**
THE PROBLEM

**Headline:**
Early-stage information is a private privilege.

**Body:**
The earliest stage of private markets runs on fragmented, unverified, socially gated information. Professional research barely exists below Series B. Founders pitch into silence. Capable analysts have no venue where rigorous work on early companies is recognized. And the distributed knowledge of operators — the people who actually know whether a technology is real — never enters the record.

The result: the stage where information matters most is the stage with the least infrastructure for it.

---

## SECTION 03 — THE VISION

**Eyebrow:**
THE VISION

**Headline:**
Venture intelligence as public infrastructure.

**Body:**
VENT's premise is that startup evaluation can be an open discipline: published methodology, structured community input weighted by earned Reputation, professional editorial standards, and technology-level IP examination — producing profiles and scores anyone serious can interrogate.

On that foundation, a professional investment layer can be built properly. That's the long arc: infrastructure first, capital second.

## SECTION 04 — THE APPROACH

**Eyebrow:**
THE APPROACH

**Headline:**
Three disciplines, deliberately combined.

**Cards:**
- **Professional research** — Fourteen-section profiles, eight scored dimensions, editorial review, published methodology.
- **Community intelligence** — Structured assessment from members, analysts, and verified experts — attributed, weighted, and always distinguishable from professional research.
- **IP forensics** — Defensibility examined at the technology level through PatentChain: prior art, ownership, landscape, evidence.

**Body:**
Around them, the VENT utility token structures participation — access, membership, governance, rewards — so contribution is recognized and the network compounds.

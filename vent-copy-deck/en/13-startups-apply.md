## SECTION 04 — MARKET

**Section intro:** The problem, the buyer, and the size of the opportunity.

- **Field: Problem** — Label: The problem you solve
  - Placeholder: Who has this problem, and what does it cost them today?
  - Helper: 2–4 sentences. Specific pain beats big vision here.
  - Error: Describe the problem (min 100 characters).
- **Field: Customer** — Label: Target customer — Placeholder: e.g., data-center operators running AI clusters · Error: Describe your target customer.
- **Field: Market size** — Label: Market size and how you calculated it
  - Placeholder: Bottom-up preferred: units × price × reachable share, with sources.
  - Helper: We verify sizing logic. "$X trillion market" without a method scores poorly.
  - Error: Describe your market size and method.

## SECTION 05 — PRODUCT

- **Field: Product description** — Label: What you've built
  - Placeholder: What exists today, who uses it, and what it does for them.
  - Helper: Describe the current state, not the roadmap. The roadmap has its own question.
  - Error: Describe your product (min 100 characters).
- **Field: Stage** — Label: Product stage — Options: Concept · Prototype · Beta · Live · Live with revenue — Error: Select a stage.
- **Field: Demo** — Label: Demo link (optional) — Placeholder: https:// — Helper: Video, sandbox, or TestFlight — anything an analyst can open.
- **Field: Roadmap** — Label: Next 12 months (optional) — Placeholder: The 3–5 milestones that matter most.

## SECTION 06 — TECHNOLOGY

**Section intro:** What's under the hood — and what makes it hard to copy.

- **Field: Technology summary** — Label: Core technology
  - Placeholder: Architecture, key components, and what you build vs. buy.

  - Error: Describe your core technology.
- **Field: Differentiation** — Label: What's genuinely hard to replicate?
  - Placeholder: Proprietary methods, data, integrations, or physics — and why they hold.
  - Helper: "First mover" and "network effects" need evidence to count.
  - Error: Describe your differentiation.

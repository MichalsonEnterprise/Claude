## SECTION 04 — WHAT HOLDERS DO NOT CONTROL

**Eyebrow:**
OUT OF SCOPE

**Headline:**
Said plainly, because it matters.

**Body:**
VENT governance is not investment management. Holders do not:

- decide what any fund or vehicle invests in,
- control any portfolio or its management,
- direct investor capital,
- decide on exits or divestments,
- set terms of any future offering.

Any such decisions would belong exclusively to the professional Investment Committee and governing bodies of the relevant future vehicle, under its own legal framework. A governance system that pretended otherwise would be mis-selling both the token and the fund. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

## SECTION 11 — FAQ

**Q: Are governance votes binding?**
A: They are advisory with published follow-through. Operational teams implement accepted proposals or publish reasons when they don't. Nothing in governance binds investment decisions of any vehicle. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: Why not full binding on-chain governance?**
A: Because the ecosystem includes regulated ambitions where law assigns responsibility to accountable people. Pretending a token vote can carry fiduciary duty would be false — we'd rather scope honestly.

**Q: Who can propose?**
A: COUNCIL and BLACK members hold proposal rights; every member can discuss and vote per their weight.

**Q: Can whales dominate votes?**
A: Weight blends locked VENT with Reputation — [PLACEHOLDER: formula]. Pure capital without contribution history has capped influence by design.

**Q: Does voting affect what VENT Ventures I invests in?**
A: No. Community output can recommend companies for consideration. Investment decisions would rest solely with the vehicle's professional Investment Committee. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

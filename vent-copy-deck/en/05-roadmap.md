## SECTION 02 — HOW TO READ THIS ROADMAP

**Eyebrow:**
THE LOGIC

**Headline:**
Each phase earns the right to the next one.

**Body:**
This roadmap is deliberately ordered. A venture platform without research infrastructure is a brand; research without a network is a newsletter; and an investment vehicle without dealflow, methodology, and track record is a risk nobody should fund. VENT builds the dependencies in order — which is why the ventures layer comes last, not first.

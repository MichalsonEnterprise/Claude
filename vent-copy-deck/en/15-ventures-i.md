## SECTION 15 — FAQ

**Q: Can I invest in VENT Ventures I today?**
A: No. The vehicle is in preparation; no capital raising is open. The waitlist is the only formal step available, and it confers nothing beyond updates. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: Does holding VENT get me into the STO?**
A: No. Any STO would have separately determined eligibility under securities regulation. Eligible holders may be offered defined ecosystem conveniences — such as reduced platform or administrative fees — never discounted securities or guaranteed allocations. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: How is this different from the token I can acquire in the distribution?**
A: Completely. The VENT token is ecosystem utility: access, membership, governance, rewards. Any Ventures I participation would be a separate, regulated instrument with its own documentation and investors. One never converts into, or entitles you to, the other. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: Who decides what Ventures I invests in?**
A: Its professional Investment Committee, under the vehicle's mandate. The ecosystem contributes scored candidates and advisory recommendations — input, not authority.

**Q: When will it launch?**
A: Preparation follows roughly twelve months of ecosystem development, with structuring in Phase 05 of the roadmap (Q4 2027+). Firm dates will come only from the vehicle's own documentation. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: What if it never launches?**
A: Then the ecosystem continues as what it already is: a venture intelligence platform. Ventures I is the planned next stage, not a condition of the ecosystem's existence — and the token's utility does not depend on it.

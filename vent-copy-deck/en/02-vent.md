## SECTION 02 — WHAT IS VENT

**Eyebrow:**
DEFINITION

**Headline:**
One token, thirteen functions, zero financial promises.

**Body:**
VENT is the utility token of the VENT ecosystem. It exists so the network can function: gating research access, weighting advisory governance, anchoring membership, rewarding contribution, and paying for ecosystem services.

It is deliberately not designed as a financial product. It carries no right to profits, no claim on assets, and no automatic path into any future offering. Its value to you is what you can do with it.

## SECTION 03 — WHY VENT EXISTS

**Eyebrow:**
PURPOSE

**Headline:**
A network this ambitious needs an economic layer.

**Body:**

**Eyebrow:**
VENT LOCK

**Headline:**
Commitment, made legible.

**Body:**

**Lock table:**

| Period | Ecosystem rewards (working target) | Additional effects |
|---|---|---|

| 180 days | up to ~4% annualized, in VENT | Membership boost + earlier access |
| 365 days | up to ~6% annualized, in VENT | Full membership weight + expanded governance influence |

## SECTION 16 — FAQ

**Q: Is buying VENT an investment?**
A: No. VENT is a utility token — you acquire it to use the ecosystem: research access, membership, governance, services, and rewards for contribution. It is not designed to generate profit, and its market value, if any, is not guaranteed. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: Does VENT give me a share of VENT Ventures I?**
A: No. Ventures I would be a separate legal vehicle with separately determined investors and eligibility. The token represents no equity, no fund share, and no allocation rights. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: Is VENT Lock staking?**
A: We don't call it that, because the term implies a yield product. VENT Lock is a commitment mechanism: locked tokens raise membership standing and governance weight, and accrue ecosystem rewards from a fixed pool. Parameters are published targets, not promises. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: What happens to VENT paid for services?**
A: Under the working model, 75% flows to the ecosystem treasury and 25% is burned — a token consumption mechanism that ties supply to actual usage. It is not operated as a price-support mechanism. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: Can I lose access if I unlock?**
A: Membership recalculates when locks end. Your Reputation is yours permanently; tier benefits tied to locked VENT adjust to your current locked balance.

**Q: Where do rewards come from?**
A: From the Ecosystem & Lock Rewards allocation (20% of supply) and the Research & Scout Rewards allocation (8%) — fixed pools created at genesis. No new tokens are minted to pay rewards.

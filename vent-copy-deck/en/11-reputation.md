## SECTION 09 — ANTI-GAMING

**Eyebrow:**
INTEGRITY

**Headline:**
Designed to resist farming.

**Anti-gaming principles:**
- **Acceptance-gated.** Only reviewed, accepted contributions earn points — volume without quality earns nothing.
- **Diminishing repetition.** Repetitive low-substance activity decays in value.
- **No transfers, no markets.** Reputation cannot move between identities; attempted sale is a violation.
- **Sybil resistance.** One verified identity per member. [PLACEHOLDER: identity mechanism]

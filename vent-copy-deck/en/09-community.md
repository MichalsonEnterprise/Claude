## SECTION 03 — COMMUNITY INTELLIGENCE

**Eyebrow:**
COMMUNITY INTELLIGENCE

**Headline:**
Individual judgment, aggregated into signal.

**Body:**
Members assess companies through structured instruments — not comment threads. Assessments roll up into Community Scores; open questions become advisory votes; contributed information routes to the research team with attribution.

**What members do (cards):**
- **Assess startups** — Structured evaluations across defined dimensions.
- **Contribute information** — Domain knowledge, diligence leads, corrections.
- **Submit startups** — Source companies; earn scout rewards when they pass screening.
- **Create analyses** — Publish research under the Community Analyst Program.
- **Discuss projects** — Structured discussion attached to profiles, on the record.
- **Vote on research direction** — Advisory votes on sectors and priorities.

**Example vote module:**
> Should VENT Research advance this company to full due diligence? **YES / NO** — advisory

**Microcopy:**
Community output is advisory. It informs research and produces recommendations — it does not direct investment decisions of any vehicle. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

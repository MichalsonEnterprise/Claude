## SECTION 24 — FAQ

**Eyebrow:**
QUESTIONS, ANSWERED PLAINLY

**Headline:**
What people ask first.

**FAQ items:**

**Q: What is VENT?**
A: VENT is a venture intelligence ecosystem — a platform where startups are discovered, screened, researched, and scored by professional analysts together with a structured community. A utility token, also called VENT, powers access, membership, governance, and rewards within it.

**Q: Is VENT a fund or an investment?**
A: No. VENT is not a fund, and the VENT token is not an investment product. It is a utility token for using the ecosystem. A separate vehicle, VENT Ventures I, is planned for the future — with its own legal structure and its own rules. The token does not represent any share in it. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: What does the VENT token actually do?**
A: It unlocks full research access, determines membership tiers together with Reputation, carries advisory governance weight, powers VENT Lock, pays for selected ecosystem services, and rewards accepted contribution — from a fixed rewards allocation.

**Q: Does the community decide what gets invested in?**
A: No. Community assessments and votes are advisory — they shape research priorities and produce recommendations. Decisions of any future investment vehicle would rest solely with its professional Investment Committee.

**Q: What is VENT Lock?**
A: A commitment mechanism. Lock VENT for 90, 180, or 365 days to raise your membership standing, gain earlier access, increase governance weight, and receive ecosystem rewards from a pre-allocated pool. It is not a savings or yield product. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: What is VENT Ventures I?**
A: A planned, separate venture investment vehicle intended to build on the ecosystem's dealflow and research after roughly twelve months of ecosystem development. It would have its own structure, documentation, and investor onboarding — including any future STO. Holding VENT does not grant participation rights. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: How do founders get involved?**
A: Apply through /startups. Standard applications are free; Accelerated Review is a paid service that shortens the screening queue — it never improves a score.

**Q: Where can I verify your claims?**
A: The Transparency page lists supply, allocations, vesting, contracts, audits, and documents — each with a live value or an honest "not yet" status.

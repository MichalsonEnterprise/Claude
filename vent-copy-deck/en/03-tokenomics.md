## SECTION 02 — TOKEN OVERVIEW

**Eyebrow:**
OVERVIEW

**Headline:**
Designed for use, not for speculation.

**Body:**
VENT is the utility token of the venture intelligence ecosystem. Its token model has three design rules:

1. **Fixed supply.** 1,000,000,000 VENT, ever. No uncontrolled minting, no elastic emissions.
2. **Funded rewards.** Every reward — lock, research, scout — is paid from pools allocated at genesis, so incentives can't silently dilute holders.
3. **Published movement.** Allocations, vesting, treasury activity, and burns are disclosed on the Transparency page.

**Eyebrow:**
SUPPLY

**Headline:**

## SECTION 08 — REWARDS ARCHITECTURE

**Eyebrow:**
REWARDS

**Headline:**
Incentives with a visible budget.

**Body:**
Two pools fund all ecosystem incentives:

- **Ecosystem & Lock Rewards (20%)** — VENT Lock rewards, participation rewards, and community programs.
- **Research & Scout Rewards (8%)** — accepted analyst work, startup sourcing, and research contribution.

Both pools are created at genesis and drawn down transparently. When a pool's emission schedule is published, actual distribution can be audited against it. No reward is ever minted into existence.

## SECTION 09 — VENT LOCK ECONOMICS

**Eyebrow:**
VENT LOCK

**Headline:**
Longer commitment, larger participation.

**Body:**
VENT Lock pays ecosystem rewards from the fixed rewards pool, scaled by lock duration. The working parameters:

| Lock period | Ecosystem rewards (working target) | Ecosystem effects |
|---|---|---|
| 90 days | up to ~2% annualized, in VENT | Membership boost |
| 180 days | up to ~4% annualized, in VENT | Membership boost + earlier access |
| 365 days | up to ~6% annualized, in VENT | Full membership weight + expanded governance influence |

**Body (continued):**
Because the budget is fixed, reward rates are targets subject to pool capacity and published adjustments — the sustainable version of an incentive, rather than an unsustainable promise.

**Microcopy:**

## SECTION 13 — BURN

**Eyebrow:**
TOKEN CONSUMPTION

**Headline:**
Usage consumes supply. By design.

**Body:**
Selected ecosystem services are paid in VENT. Under the working model, each payment splits 75% to the treasury and 25% to burn — permanently removing that VENT from supply.

This is a token consumption mechanism: it ties total supply to real ecosystem usage and makes service demand visible on-chain. It is an economic design choice, not a price commitment, and we make no claims about its market effects.

**Microcopy:**
Burn events will be published on the Transparency page. Split parameters are working values. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

## SECTION 19 — FAQ

**Q: Can more VENT ever be minted?**
A: No. Maximum supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 at genesis. All rewards and programs are funded from allocations inside that cap.

**Q: Where do lock rewards come from?**
A: From the Ecosystem & Lock Rewards pool — 20% of supply, created at genesis. Rewards draw the pool down; they never mint new tokens.

**Q: Why is the community allocation the largest?**
A: Because the ecosystem's core product — community intelligence layered on professional research — only works with broad, engaged distribution. Concentrated ownership would defeat the design.

**Q: What stops the team from selling early?**
A: Vesting: a 12-month cliff, then 36 months of linear release. The schedule will be published and, where feasible, enforced on-chain. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

**Q: Is the burn meant to raise the price?**
A: The burn ties supply to service usage — a consumption mechanism, published transparently. We make no claims about market effects, and the token is not designed as a financial product.

**Q: Are these numbers final?**
A: They are the working model. Final parameters will be fixed in the MiCA white paper and distribution documentation, and any changes will be published before TGE. `[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]`

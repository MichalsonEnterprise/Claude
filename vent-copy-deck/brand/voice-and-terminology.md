# VENT — Voice & Terminology

The reference document for anyone writing or reviewing VENT copy. If copy conflicts with this file, this file wins.

---

## 1. Brand personality

Institutional. Intelligent. Ambitious. Credible. Premium. International. Minimal. Technology-forward. Confident.

The register to aim for: a top-tier venture firm's website crossed with an institutional market-intelligence product. Not a crypto launchpad. Not a trading community. Never a countdown clock.

## 2. Voice principles

1. **Say less, mean more.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns. One idea per section. If a sentence would survive on any ICO website, cut it.
2. **Precision over promotion.** We describe what exists, what is being built, and what is planned — and we label each one as such. The discipline itself is the brand.
3. **Verifiable or absent.** No claim without a basis. No statistic without a source. Where data doesn't exist yet, we show a designed pre-launch state, not an invented number.
4. **Confidence without promises.** We are building toward a professional venture platform. We never promise returns, token price outcomes, fund success, or access to future financial products.
5. **Respect the reader.** Founders, analysts, and professional investors read this site. Write up to them, never down. No exclamation marks. No urgency mechanics.
6. **Separation is sacred.** The VENT utility token and the future VENT Ventures I vehicle are different things with different rules. Every page keeps that boundary visible.

## 4. Banned vocabulary (all languages, all pages)

**Hype:** revolutionary · game-changing · disruptive (as self-praise) · 100x · moon · massive returns · once-in-a-lifetime · next Bitcoin · financial freedom · don't miss out · last chance · FOMO mechanics of any kind

**Financial promises:** guaranteed (in any financial context) · guaranteed APY · passive income · risk-free · earn yield · profit potential · ROI · returns (about the token) · investment opportunity (about the utility token) · "price will rise" · "buy before it's more expensive"

**Category errors:** staking (in primary copy; one clarifying FAQ entry is permitted) · dividend · equity (as something VENT grants) · share of the fund · deal access (for research timing) · "holders decide what the fund invests in" · "discounted shares" · "guaranteed allocation"

**Empty corporate filler:** unlock synergies · best-in-class (unverifiable) · world-class (unverifiable) · cutting-edge (as decoration) · "Learn More" as a CTA label

## 5. Register calibration

- **Headlines:** 3–9 words. Declarative. No punctuation tricks.
- **Subheadlines:** one sentence, max two lines, adds information the headline didn't carry.
- **Body:** 40–120 words per section. Paragraphs of one to three sentences.
- **Cards:** title 2–4 words; description 12–28 words.
- **FAQs:** direct answers in the first sentence, context after.
- **Disclaimers:** plain, calm, unhedged. A confident project states its limits clearly.

## 6. The one-sentence separations (reuse-safe)

These express the two boundaries the whole site depends on. Vary the wording page to page; keep the meaning identical.

- **Advisory boundary:** "The community recommends. A professional Investment Committee decides."
- **Structural boundary:** "The VENT token powers the ecosystem. VENT Ventures I — a separate, future vehicle — would hold investments. One never represents the other."

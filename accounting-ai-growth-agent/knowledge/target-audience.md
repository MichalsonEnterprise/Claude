# Accounting AI — Target Audience

## Primary segments

1. **Właściciele małych i średnich firm w Polsce** (SMB owners) who handle or
   oversee their own accounting/compliance exposure and are cost- and
   time-sensitive.
2. **Księgowi i biura rachunkowe** (accountants / accounting offices) looking
   for tools that reduce manual document handling and reconciliation load.
3. **CFOs / finance leads** at growing companies evaluating whether to modernize
   their accounting stack.

## What they care about

- Not missing legal deadlines (VAT, CIT, PIT, JPK, KSeF).
- Reducing manual, repetitive document work.
- Catching errors before they become penalties.
- Trusting that a tool won't create legal/compliance risk.

## What makes them skeptical

- Overpromising AI ("AI zrobi to za Ciebie w 100%").
- Vague claims with no specifics.
- Tools that look like they were built by people who don't understand Polish
  tax law.

## Tone that lands with them

Concrete, specific, slightly technical, respectful of their expertise.

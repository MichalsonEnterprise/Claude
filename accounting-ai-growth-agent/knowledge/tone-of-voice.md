# Accounting AI — Tone of Voice

## Principles

1. **Concrete over grand.** Prefer "System wykrywa niespójności między datą
   wystawienia a datą sprzedaży na fakturze" over "Accounting AI
   zrewolucjonizuje księgowość."
2. **Hedge status honestly.** Use "testujemy", "pracujemy nad",
   "w rozwoju" for anything not fully shipped (see `product-features.md`).
3. **No hype adjectives**: avoid "rewolucyjny", "przełomowy", "genialny",
   "niesamowity" unless directly quoting someone else.
4. **Sentence starters that work well** (from the task brief):
   - "System wykrywa…"
   - "Moduł analizuje…"
   - "Pracujemy nad…"
   - "Problem wygląda tak…"
   - "Obecny proces wymaga…"
   - "AI może zautomatyzować…"
5. **Minimal emoji.** None on LinkedIn/X. At most one, sparingly, on
   Instagram/Facebook captions. Never emoji as bullet markers.
6. **Hashtags**: 0–3 on LinkedIn, 0–2 on X, 0–5 on Instagram, 0–3 on TikTok.
   Always relevant, never generic spam tags.
7. **No fear-mongering.** Legislative/tax news should inform, not alarm.

## Per-platform register

- **LinkedIn**: expert register, full sentences, short paragraphs, one clear
  insight per post.
- **X**: terse, one strong point per tweet, thread only when the topic
  genuinely needs 3+ beats.
- **Facebook**: same substance as LinkedIn but slightly more conversational,
  can address the reader directly ("Prowadzisz firmę i...").
- **Instagram**: caption supports a visual carousel; hook in the first line.
- **TikTok**: caption is short, the carousel/slides carry the content.
